Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to CampusHinge ("we," "our," or "us"). CampusHinge is a campus-verified dating platform that connects students exclusively from verified educational institutions. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data with care and full transparency.

This Privacy Policy describes in detail how we collect, use, store, share, and safeguard your personal information when you access and use our platform at www.campushinge.online (the "Platform"). By using CampusHinge, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information Obtained Through Google Sign-In

CampusHinge uses Google OAuth 2.0 for authentication. When you sign in using your Google account, we request and receive the following information from Google:

  • Email address — We use your email address to verify that you belong to a supported campus (e.g., vitbhopal.ac.in, lpu.co.in, bits-pilani.ac.in). Only emails from approved educational institution domains are accepted.
  • Full name — Your name as registered with Google, used as your default display name on the Platform.
  • Profile picture URL — Your Google profile photo, optionally used as your default avatar on the Platform.
  • Google account identifier (sub) — A unique identifier used solely for authentication and to link your Google account to your CampusHinge account.

Scope limitation: We only request the minimum OAuth scopes necessary: openid, email, and profile. We do not request or access your Google contacts, Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Google Calendar, Google Photos, or any other Google services or data beyond basic profile information.

2.2 Profile Information You Voluntarily Provide

When you create or update your CampusHinge profile, you may voluntarily provide:

  • Display name and bio — Shown to other users on your profile card
  • Age and date of birth — Used to display your age and ensure eligibility (18+)
  • Gender and gender preferences — Used for matching preferences
  • College or university name — Displayed on your profile to establish campus affiliation
  • Profile photographs — Photos you upload to be displayed on your profile
  • Interests, hobbies, and prompts — Optional information to enhance your profile

2.3 Usage and Activity Data

We automatically collect certain information as you interact with the Platform:

  • Swipe activity — Records of profiles you have liked or passed on, used to generate matches and prevent duplicate recommendations
  • Match data — Records of mutual likes (matches) between users
  • Chat messages — Messages you send and receive through the Platform's messaging feature, stored to enable message delivery and conversation history
  • Session information — Login timestamps, session duration, and authentication tokens
  • Device and browser information — Browser type, operating system, and screen resolution (collected automatically via standard HTTP headers)

2.4 Information We Do Not Collect

CampusHinge does not collect:

  • Precise geolocation or GPS data
  • Financial or payment card information directly (payments are handled by third-party processors)
  • Contacts or address book data from your device
  • Biometric data (fingerprints, facial recognition, etc.)
  • Data from other apps on your device

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Campus verification: To verify that your email belongs to a supported educational institution domain and confirm your eligibility to use the Platform
  • Account creation and management: To create your account, authenticate your identity, and maintain your profile
  • Profile display: To show your profile information (name, photos, bio, college) to other verified campus users as part of the discovery and matching experience
  • Matching and discovery: To recommend profiles, process swipe actions, and create matches based on mutual interest
  • Messaging: To deliver chat messages between matched users and maintain conversation history
  • Notifications: To send you important account-related notifications such as new matches, messages, and account security alerts
  • Platform improvement: To understand how users interact with the Platform, identify issues, and improve features and user experience
  • Safety and fraud prevention: To detect and prevent fraudulent accounts, spam, harassment, and other prohibited activities
  • Legal compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

CampusHinge does not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise commercialize your personal data to any third party. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With other platform users: Your profile information (display name, photos, bio, college, age) is visible to other verified users on the Platform as part of the core matching and discovery functionality. Your email address is never shared with other users.
  • With service providers: We use trusted third-party infrastructure providers to operate the Platform, including Cloudflare (hosting, CDN, database), Vercel (frontend delivery), and Google (authentication). These providers process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality and data protection obligations and only as necessary to provide their services.
  • For payment processing: If you purchase a premium subscription, payment information is processed directly by Razorpay. We do not store your credit card or bank details.
  • For legal compliance: We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, subpoena, or governmental regulation, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of CampusHinge, our users, or the public.
  • With your consent: We may share information for other purposes if you provide explicit consent.

5. Data Storage, Retention, and Security

5.1 Storage

Your data is stored on Cloudflare's global infrastructure, which provides enterprise-grade security and reliability. Profile photos and media are stored in Cloudflare R2 object storage. Structured data (profiles, matches, messages) is stored in Cloudflare D1 databases.

5.2 Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Platform services. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain certain information.

5.3 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS protocols
  • Secure authentication: JWT (JSON Web Token) based authentication with secure token handling and expiration policies
  • Access controls: Strict access controls ensure only authorized systems and personnel can access user data
  • Infrastructure security: Cloudflare's enterprise security infrastructure includes DDoS protection, Web Application Firewall (WAF), and continuous monitoring
  • Regular reviews: We conduct periodic security assessments and update our practices to address emerging threats

While we implement robust security measures, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access: You can view all personal data stored in your CampusHinge profile at any time by accessing your account
  • Right to correction: You can update or correct your profile information at any time through the Platform's profile settings
  • Right to deletion: You can request deletion of your account and all associated personal data by contacting us at dd961847@gmail.com. Account deletion is processed within 30 days
  • Right to data portability: You can request a copy of your personal data in a machine-readable format by contacting us
  • Right to revoke Google permissions: You can revoke CampusHinge's access to your Google account data at any time by visiting myaccount.google.com/permissions and removing CampusHinge from the list of connected apps
  • Right to withdraw consent: You may withdraw your consent to data processing at any time by deleting your account. Note that withdrawing consent may affect your ability to use the Platform

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at dd961847@gmail.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

7. Cookies, Local Storage, and Tracking

CampusHinge uses browser local storage to maintain your authentication session (JWT token). This is essential for the Platform to function and keep you signed in.

We do not use third-party tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics tracking tools. We do not serve targeted advertisements.

Google's sign-in widget may set its own cookies as part of the authentication process. These cookies are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

9. Children's Privacy and Age Requirement

CampusHinge is intended exclusively for college and university students who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect, use, or store personal information from individuals under 18 years of age.

If we discover that we have inadvertently collected data from a user under 18, we will promptly delete that user's account and all associated data. If you believe a minor is using the Platform, please contact us immediately at dd961847@gmail.com.
